FAQ

Questions? We've got answers

How do you decide which meal plan is right for me?

Based on your age, height, weight, goal, activity level and food preferences, our team calculates your calorie and protein needs and recommends the best plan and portion sizes for you.

What's the difference between 20, 24 and 30 day subscriptions?

It's the number of delivery days per month. 20 days covers weekdays only, 24 days is 6 days a week, and 30 days means meals every single day.

Can I choose fixed meals or a different menu daily?

Yes! You can choose the same meals every day (great for strict tracking) or a rotating menu with different meals daily.

Do you cater to allergies and special diets?

Absolutely. We offer Regular, High Protein, Low Carb, Keto and Vegetarian options, and we avoid any ingredients or allergens you tell us about.

When will my meals be delivered?

Meals are delivered fresh daily within your preferred time slot to your delivery area.
